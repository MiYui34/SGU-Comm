# Streaming: previews and viewer list

## Requirements

- People not watching see a small, rarely updated thumbnail of each stream; the streamer can hide it (setting and a checkbox at the start).
- The streamer sees who is watching.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Server tests for the viewer list; checked in the browser.

## Notes

- Previews: a small JPEG from the streamer about every 15 s (turned off in the streamer's settings), kept by the server and sent only to people who may enter the stream's channel.
