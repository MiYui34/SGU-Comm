# Who can read a private channel

## Summary

In a private channel the member list shows who can read it, so people
know who they write for: its members, and the moderators and admins
(who see every private channel), each group with a heading
("Members", "Staff"). Online and offline as in the normal list.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Server: the readers of a private channel (members + staff) go to its
  readers only; updates on member and role changes; tests.
- Web: the member list of a private text channel shows them; tests for
  the grouping.
