# Web: play audio and video attachments inline

## Summary

Audio and video uploads show only as file cards; they should play in the chat.

## Requirements

- Audio: the file card (name, size, download) with a player row: play/pause, time, seek bar, mute (mockup A).
- Video: the video with a play button and duration before it starts, controls while it plays, and a bar below with name, size and download.
- Seeking works (Range requests); only one plays at a time.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Checked in the browser with an MP3 and an MP4 upload.
