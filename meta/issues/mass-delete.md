# Mass delete

## Summary

Moderators and admins remove a user's recent messages in one step: in
the user menu, "Delete messages" with a time range (last hour, 24 hours,
7 days), in all channels. Also offered with kick, ban and time-out, as
Discord does. Staff messages are safe from moderators.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Server tests: staff only; the range; all channels and their uploads;
  clients get the removals; moderators cannot delete staff messages.
- Web: the menu item with a confirmation; the option in the ban dialog.
